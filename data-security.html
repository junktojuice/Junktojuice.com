Important to know
What "physical destruction" means
Physical destruction applies specifically to storage media — hard drives, SSDs, flash memory chips, and the storage inside phones and tablets. The drive is drilled through or physically shredded, making data unrecoverable by any means. The rest of the device (circuit boards, plastics, metals, display) is recycled normally — we're not destroying the whole laptop, just the part that holds data.
Software wipe uses DoD 5220.22-M compliant overwriting tools (DBAN, Eraser) that overwrite all sectors of a drive multiple times. This is sufficient for most business compliance needs. Physical destruction is recommended for highly sensitive environments like healthcare, legal, and financial services.
