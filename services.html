What we take

If it plugs in, it's worth asking about.

We pick up retired electronics from businesses across central NC, free. Households welcome too. Not sure about something? Text a photo.

Office and IT

  • Desktops, laptops & thin clients
  • Servers & storage
  • Switches, routers & firewalls
  • Monitors & TVs
  • Printers, copiers & scanners
  • Phones, VoIP & tablets
  • UPS units & power gear
  • Hard drives, RAM & parts
  • Cables, keyboards & peripherals

Specialty equipment

  • Lab & test instruments
  • Medical & dental electronics
  • Industrial controls & drives
  • Professional AV & projectors
  • Telecom systems
  • Security & access control
  • Point-of-sale systems
  • Signage & displays

At home

  • Computers & laptops
  • TVs & monitors
  • Game consoles
  • Phones & tablets
  • Printers
  • Cords & chargers

Working or not

Broken and untested equipment is fine. Dead machines still have recyclable parts.

No sorting needed

Leave it as it is. We sort it after pickup.

Resale value?

If anything in the lot has resale value, we'll make an offer.

One thing to ask about first

Old tube TVs and CRT monitors cost money to recycle properly. A few mixed in with a load are no problem; if that's most of what you have, text a photo first and we'll talk it through.
