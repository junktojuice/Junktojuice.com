How it works

Two ways to get rid of it. Zero hassle.

Pickup is always free in central NC. Drop-off by appointment. If it plugs in or has a battery, we probably want it — broken or not.

Your options
Two ways to get rid of it

We come to you

Schedule a free pickup at your home or office. We haul it away — no effort on your end.

Drop it off

Drop-off is available by appointment. Give us a call or send a message and we'll set a time that works — we're flexible on location for the right load.
