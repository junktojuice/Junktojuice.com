The process
How paid acquisition works
We built this process to protect both sides. You know what you're getting before we pick anything up.
1

Submit a photo manifest

Send us photos and a rough inventory — make, model, condition, quantity. You don't need a perfect spreadsheet, just enough for us to price honestly.

2

We provide a tiered quote

We'll give you a range by grade (A/B/C/scrap) based on the manifest. You'll know your floor and ceiling before we ever show up.

3

On-site grading & sight adjustment

When we arrive, we physically assess and grade each device. If the actual condition differs significantly from the manifest, we discuss it before finalizing — no surprises at pickup.

4

Payment + documentation

Payment for qualified equipment, certificate of destruction for data-bearing devices, serialized inventory, and landfill diversion report — all in one transaction.

How we grade
Four tiers. Priced honestly.
Every device gets assessed against these criteria on-site. Tier determines what we pay — or whether the item becomes a recycling-only piece with no charge to you.
Grade A

Resale-ready

Powers on, no major cosmetic damage, all components present. Likely candidate for direct resale or refurbishment.

Highest payout
Grade B

Functional with flaws

Powers on and functional, but cosmetic damage, missing accessories, or wear that reduces resale value. Still has positive value.

Moderate payout
Grade C

Parts / low value

Functional components but limited as a complete unit. May have value for parts. Payout depends heavily on make and model.

Low / case-by-case payout
Scrap

Material value only

Non-functional, heavily damaged, or obsolete. Value comes from metals and materials only. Recycled at no cost to you.

Recycled free — no charge
Built-in protections
How we keep the deal fair for both sides
Paying for equipment means taking on risk. These are the structures we use to make sure neither party gets burned.
📸

Photo manifest first

We never commit a price without seeing what you have. Photos and a rough list come before any quote.

🔍

Sight-adjustment clause

If the on-site condition meaningfully differs from the manifest, we discuss before finalizing. No one is locked into a bad deal.

💰

Capped exposure per deal

For new relationships, we cap total acquisition spend per transaction until we've established trust with your inventory quality.

📊

Floor price awareness

Our quotes are anchored to real resale and scrap market prices — we don't speculate, and we're transparent about what drives the number.

🚫

Disposal-cost items excluded

CRTs, certain batteries, and high-hazmat items don't qualify for paid acquisition — these cost money to process and we'll tell you upfront.

📋

Full chain-of-custody docs

Certificate of destruction, serialized inventory, and ESG diversion report included — regardless of whether we paid or recycled free.
