Surplus Electronics Buyer Serving Central North Carolina

Junk to Juice is based in Sanford and buys surplus, decommissioned and end-of-life electronics from businesses across central NC. Send us a list or a few photos and we'll tell you what it's worth, wherever you are in the areas below.

Sanford & Lee County

Our home base. We buy from manufacturers, labs, offices, schools and contractors in Sanford, Broadway, and the rest of Lee County, plus nearby Pittsboro and Cameron. Equipment that has been sitting in a storeroom or warehouse is exactly what we look for.

Raleigh, Cary & Apex

Office refreshes, server and network decommissions, AV upgrades and lab clean-outs across the Triangle. We provide itemized offers, documented data destruction and certificates your IT or compliance team can file.

Fayetteville & Fort Bragg

We buy surplus IT, telecom, medical and industrial equipment from businesses, contractors and medical practices in Fayetteville and the Fort Bragg area. Every drive is sanitized or destroyed before anything is resold.

Pinehurst & Southern Pines

We buy retired equipment from medical offices, clinics, hospitality and small businesses in Moore County, turning it into cash and getting it out of the way, with data destruction included.
