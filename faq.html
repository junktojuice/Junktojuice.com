FAQ

Common questions

Don't see yours? Text (919) 346-3172 or email Steve@junktojuice.com.

Is pickup really free?

Yes. The one thing to ask about first is old tube TVs and CRT monitors, which cost money to recycle properly. A few mixed into a load are fine; if that's most of what you have, text a photo first.

Do you take electronics from homes?

Yes. Most of our work is with businesses, but household electronics are welcome too. Text a photo of what you have.

What do you take?

Computers, servers, network gear, monitors, TVs, printers, phones, and plenty of specialty equipment. See the full list.

Does it have to work?

No. Broken, untested and dead equipment is fine.

Do I need to sort anything?

No. Leave it as it is and we'll sort it after pickup.

Do you pay for equipment?

If anything in the lot has resale value, we'll make an offer. Pickup is free either way.

What happens to my data?

Every drive is wiped following NIST SP 800-88 or physically destroyed, and you can get a certificate of destruction. More on the data security page.

What areas do you serve?

Sanford and Lee County, plus Raleigh, Cary, Apex, Fayetteville, Fort Bragg, Pinehurst and Southern Pines.

What happens to everything you collect?

Working equipment is reused, parts are recovered, and the rest goes to responsible recyclers. Nothing goes to a landfill.
