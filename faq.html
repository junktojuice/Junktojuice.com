Data & security
All storage devices — hard drives, SSDs, phones, tablets — are either securely wiped or physically destroyed before anything leaves our hands. We don't just delete files; we use methods that make data unrecoverable. If you need a certificate of destruction for compliance purposes, just ask.
Yes. If you're a business that needs documentation for compliance — whether for HIPAA, PCI-DSS, or internal IT policy — we can provide a certificate of destruction for your records. This is especially useful for companies disposing of servers, laptops, and hard drives in bulk. Just let us know when you reach out.
You don't have to — we handle that. But if it gives you peace of mind to do a factory reset first, go for it. Either way, we treat every device as if it still has sensitive data on it.
No — physical destruction applies specifically to the storage media inside the device: the hard drive, SSD, or flash memory chip. That component is drilled through or shredded so data is unrecoverable. The rest of the device — circuit boards, plastics, metals, display — is recycled normally. You get a certificate documenting exactly what was destroyed.
