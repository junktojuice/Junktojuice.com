Data & security
It's destroyed before the equipment goes anywhere near resale. Drives are sanitized to NIST SP 800-88 standards or physically destroyed at your direction, and that happens before a machine is tested, graded or listed. Nothing is powered up for evaluation with your data still on it, and any drive that fails sanitization verification is destroyed rather than sold.
Yes, on every commercial job, and it's included rather than billed separately. It documents what was collected, the method applied to each drive, and when. It's built for HIPAA, PCI-DSS, GLBA and internal IT compliance files.
