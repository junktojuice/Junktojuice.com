How we're different
Three things can happen to your equipment. Two of them pay you.

Most disposal vendors sort your equipment into "we'll take it" and "that'll cost you." We sort it by what it's actually worth, and you see the whole breakdown before anything moves — including an honest read on the parts that aren't worth anything.

We pay you

Equipment with resale value

Servers and business laptops, network and telecom gear, lab and medical instruments, industrial controls, professional AV, unopened overstock. We make a firm offer up front — no consignment, no waiting to see what it sells for.

We pay you

Recoverable components

Drives, memory, CPUs, GPUs, optics, cards, modules and assemblies pulled from otherwise dead equipment. A machine that won't power on still often has real value sitting inside it.

Case by case

Material with no market

CRTs, dead consumer gear, mixed cable, obsolete equipment nobody wants. Where it's a reasonable share of an otherwise good lot, it rides along at no charge. Where a load is mostly this, we may quote a handling fee or pass on it — and we'll say so before we schedule, not at the dock.

The process
From asset list to payment
1

Send us what you have

An asset list, a spreadsheet export, or just photos. If the lot is large, mixed, or awkward to describe, ask and we'll arrange a site visit and walk it with you instead.

2

We make an offer

Usually within one business day. Itemized, so you can see what's driving the number and take it to your finance team.

3

We collect and document

Serialized inventory captured at your site before anything is loaded. You keep a copy of the manifest.

4

Data destroyed, you get paid

Drives are sanitized or destroyed to your spec. Payment on pickup, or after verification for larger lots — your choice.

What doesn't change
Buying your equipment raises the bar. It doesn't lower it.

🔒 Your data still doesn't leave with your hardware

Reselling a device means it ends up in someone else's hands, which makes sanitization more critical, not less. Every storage device is wiped to NIST SP 800-88 standards or physically destroyed before a machine is tested, listed, or resold. Certificates of destruction are available on every commercial job.

🌱 Nothing we collect goes to a landfill

Everything we take is reused, harvested for parts, or routed to responsible downstream processing — including the portion we didn't pay you for. We'll document the chain on request. That commitment is also why we're honest about what we can accept: agreeing to haul material we can't responsibly handle wouldn't do anyone any favors.
